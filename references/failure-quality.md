# Failure Quality

## F4 — Evidence/Claim Mismatch

**Definition:** Evidence is promoted beyond what it directly supports.

**Signals**
- compilation/formatting becomes correctness;
- green tests become full completion;
- a small sample becomes a field/repository/history-wide claim;
- generated files become artifact-quality evidence;
- no search hit becomes evidence of novelty or absence;
- moved/compressed files become evidence of substantive cleanup;
- a monitoring/notification capability is declared operational without verified data access/delivery;
- aggregate checks stand in for independent acceptance criteria;
- universal language appears without full bounded coverage.

**Response:** Downgrade to the strongest claim supported by type-matched, discriminating, attributed, per-criterion evidence.

## F5 — Technical/Domain Correctness Failure

**Definition:** A controlling technical object is wrong or insufficiently checked.

**Signals**
- a required term is omitted;
- an invariant/metric is misidentified;
- implementation defects are interpreted as scientific results;
- a proof operation is applied incorrectly;
- conflicting numerical evidence remains unresolved;
- configuration appears adjustable while execution remains hard-coded;
- an ablation/baseline is structurally unfair or not re-fit.

**Response:** Inspect the controlling object directly. Seek the smallest counterexample or failing path before using the claim downstream.

## F6 — Semantic/Artifact/Baseline Fidelity Loss

**Definition:** Transformation weakens substantive content, requested quality, or the identity of the research object used for evaluation.

**Signals**
- novelty, mechanism, assumptions, limitations, falsification conditions, or attribution disappear;
- scholarly prose becomes project-management prose;
- full-document editing becomes keyword cleanup;
- visible artifact quality is ignored in favor of file validity;
- estimator/sample/parameter regime/research question/figure responsibility changes but is presented as an update of the original result;
- a narrowed derivative is used to judge the broader original work.

**Response:** Compare before/after at semantic, scientific-object, baseline, and visible-artifact level. Restore lost content or explicitly distinguish the new object.

## F7 — Research/Retrieval/Novelty Failure

**Definition:** Weak retrieval or frontier discrimination produces weak research conclusions or false novelty.

**Signals**
- keyword relevance is mistaken for frontier importance;
- weak comparisons replace closest direct prior work;
- ordinary engineering is framed as scientific novelty;
- narrow search becomes a broad field conclusion;
- cross-disciplinary ideation begins before either side is structurally understood;
- difference from the user's earlier draft is mistaken for novelty over literature;
- recommendations are made before candidate/resource coverage is adequate;
- vague adversarial concern creates a new research branch without a concrete evidence path.

**Response:** Reconstruct frontier and bottleneck, improve source coverage, identify direct prior work, require P-L-M-Δ, and investigate only concrete adversarial hypotheses.
